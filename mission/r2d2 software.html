Section 01

System Architecture Overview

The system is built around two processing units and one flight controller. The Myriad X VPU (inside OAK-D Lite) handles all inference. The Jetson TX2 NX runs the FSM and dispatches MAVLink. The Pixhawk 6X Mini owns all flight dynamics — exclusively.

Two processing units (Myriad X VPU + Jetson TX2 NX) and one flight controller (Pixhawk 6X Mini). TX2 NX dispatches MAVLink only — no raw inference. All PID loops and motor authority stay on Pixhawk exclusively.

Offboard Computing Integration

Due to bandwidth constraints, offboard computing is strictly limited to payload safety checks. The ground station monitors whether the payload exists and whether it has been released properly. No heavy computing (like SLAM or A-star) is performed offboard because the telemetry link cannot transmit the required dense data. All visual navigation and path planning run entirely onboard the TX2 NX.

Onboard (TX2 NX)

  • Full onboard Visual SLAM and A-star path planning
  • Transmit FC health and winch state to GCS via 3DR
  • Dynamic path updates every 30 ms

Offboard (Ground Station)

  • Payload safety monitoring (exists and released properly)
  • FC health check monitoring via 3DR link
  • Emergency RTL trigger

Backup 3DR Telemetry — Payload Safety

A pair of 3DR 915 MHz radios (one on the drone, one at the ground station) provides a secondary MAVLink connection independent of the Skydroid T12 RC link. This link serves two primary roles: FC health monitoring and payload safety checks.

During the winch sequence, the ground station receives real-time winch state telemetry — current DRV8838 current draw, ADRC swing estimates, spool position, and release confirmation — over the 3DR link. If the Skydroid T12 datalink drops during payload delivery, the 3DR radio allows the ground operator to independently command an emergency RTL via MAVLink without relying on the RC link or the TX2 NX FSM.

3DR Safety Triggers (ground station → Pixhawk via 3DR):
Payload release not confirmed within 10 s of winch activation → GCS sends RTL command.  |  Winch current anomaly (DRV8838 overcurrent) → GCS commands immediate brake + RTL.  |  Both RC and 3DR links lost → Pixhawk standalone failsafe RTL activates.

Section 02

Mission 2 Complete Sequence

Mission 2 (SkyScan) is fully autonomous. Nine phases from pre-flight to landing. No manual intervention permitted after arm.

P0
Pre-Flight and Arm
Ground · Jetson TX2 NX FSM State 0

Jetson boots, MAVLink initialised, cameras verified, GPS lock confirmed, geo-fence boundary uploaded to Pixhawk. Throttle cap 60% active.

P1
Autonomous Takeoff
0 → 5 m · MAV_CMD_NAV_TAKEOFF

Vertical ascent to 5 m. Pixhawk EKF3 holds position. No lateral movement. Altitude confirmed within 0.3 m tolerance before FSM advances.

P2
Start QR Code Scan Mandatory Gate
5 m altitude · 1 m forward · OAK-D Lite primary

Myriad X crops greyscale ROI. TX2 NX ZBar C++ decodes. target_id stored. Drone does not proceed until confirmed. On 8 consecutive failures, switches to Arducam B0251 fallback.

P3
Banner Detection and Corridor Alignment
5 m → 3 m · YOLO green class · centroid PID

Myriad X detects AeroTHON 2026 green banner. Stereo depth gives distance. Centroid PID aligns to corridor centreline within 0.25 m. Drone descends to 3 m (10 ft) for corridor entry.

P4
Corridor Navigation — Forward Lap
3 m altitude · 2 m/s ideal · Wall-following only — NO obstacle nav

Pure corridor wall-following using stereo depth centring. No obstacles on forward lap. No A-star. Depth profile samples left and right wall distances; lateral velocity correction re-centres if asymmetry exceeds 0.3 m.

P5
Delivery Zone Entry and Ascent
3 m → 10 m · Barometer + OAK-D depth blended

Exit corridor, ascend to 10 m. Altitude confirmed within 0.3 m before scanning begins.

P6
Delivery Zone QR Identification
10 m altitude · hover scan · HSV red rejection

Hover scan at 10 m. Myriad X crops QR regions. ZBar decodes each. Match against target_id. HSV red mask rejects any QR inside or within 1.5 m of a red zone. 3D world position computed. Confirmed over 3 consecutive frames.

P7
Payload Delivery + CG Compensation
10 m → 5 m → 10 m · ADRC winch · Mechanical release

Fly to position above QR at 10 m. Descend to 5 m. Zone alignment PID (0.25 m tolerance). Winch ADRC sequence: ramp lower → ground contact → mechanical-release → ramp reel-back. Post-drop: CG feed-forward correction + stability gate. Ascend to 10 m.

P8
Return Flight — Obstacle Nav Active Full Pipeline
Variable altitude · A-star on 2D occupancy grid · Visual SLAM odometry

Full OAK-D obstacle navigation active. A-star path planning on depth-derived 2D occupancy grid updated every 30 ms. Visual SLAM provides odometry to Pixhawk. Banner detection on return lap re-aligns to corridor. 2 m/s clear, 0.5 m/s dense.

P9
Landing
GPS RTL · 0.3 m/s final descent · Disarm on touchdown

GPS return to stored take-off coordinates. Pixhawk LAND mode. 0.3 m/s final approach. Motors disarm on touchdown.

Course layout. Forward lap (green): corridor wall-following only — no obstacles. Return lap (orange dashed): full A-star obstacle navigation active. Target QR is unique per team; not pre-programmed.

Section 03

Corridor Navigation — Forward vs Return Lap

Forward Lap: Wall-Following via Depth Centring

The drone follows the 3.5 m wide corridor at 3 m altitude. The Myriad X generates a depth map per frame. The TX2 NX samples the depth map at fixed horizontal slices to extract left wall distance and right wall distance. The drone is commanded to maintain equal distance from both walls.

// Lateral wall balance from depth map columns (TX2 NX CPU)
float left_dist  = median_depth_in_region(depth, LEFT_COLUMN_ROI);
float right_dist = median_depth_in_region(depth, RIGHT_COLUMN_ROI);
float lateral_err = left_dist - right_dist;   // positive = drift right
float vy_cmd      = -0.3f * lateral_err;        // push back to centre
mavsdk.send_velocity(FWD_SPEED, vy_cmd, 0.0f);

Return Lap: Full Obstacle Navigation

On return the full pipeline activates: A-star path planning on a 2D occupancy grid derived from depth segments, updated every 30 ms. Visual SLAM (RTAB-Map or NVBlox) provides odometry as a GPS substitute inside the corridor. Speed 2 m/s in clear sections, 0.5 m/s in dense sections.

Forward Lap

  • Depth centring — left/right wall balance
  • No obstacle detection active
  • No A-star path planning
  • 2 m/s, hard cap 5 m/s
  • Lowest compute load — 30 ms cycle maintained easily

Return Lap

  • Full OAK-D stereo obstacle segmentation
  • A-star on 2D occupancy grid (30 ms update)
  • Visual SLAM odometry to Pixhawk
  • 2 m/s clear, 0.5 m/s dense sections
  • Full pipeline — highest compute load
Section 04

Delivery Zone: QR Identification and Red Zone Avoidance

Multiple QR codes are scattered across the 40 m × 30 m delivery zone. The drone must identify the one matching target_id while rejecting codes inside or near the red zone. No target coordinates are pre-programmed — everything is determined in flight.

Step-by-Step Identification

Hover scan 10 m→ Myriad X QR crop→ ZBar decode + match→ Red zone reject→ 3D pos via depth→ Confirm ×3 frames→ qr_3d_pos locked

At 10 m with a 45° tilt, the OAK-D Lite sees the floor approximately 10 m ahead (tan 45° × 10 m). The drone performs a slow lateral sweep at under 1 m/s. Each decoded string is compared to target_id. The HSV red mask rejects any QR whose centroid falls within 1.5 m of a red zone cluster. 3D world position is computed via pinhole projection using depth at the QR centroid, then confirmed over three consecutive frames before the drone commits to descend.

for each frame at 10m:
  crops = myriad_x.get_qr_regions()
  for crop in crops:
    decoded = zbar.scan(crop)
    if decoded == target_id:
      if not in_red_zone(crop.bbox):
        pos = depth_to_world(crop.centroid, depth_frame)
        confirm_count += 1
        if confirm_count >= 3:
          qr_3d_pos = pos
          transition to delivery

Red Zone Detection

HSV colour segmentation uses two hue ranges to handle lighting variation: Hue 0–10 and Hue 170–180, both with Saturation above 120 and Value above 80. Red zone pixels are also overlaid on the depth safety map so the FSM avoids red areas both visually and physically.

Red Zone Rules
At 10 m scan: reject any QR whose centroid is within 1.5 m of a red cluster.
During descent: Pixhawk geo-fence exclusion zone refuses waypoint if target falls inside.
During lateral flight: Myriad X red mask + depth map → steer around within 3 m.
Section 05

ADRC Winch System

ADRC Principle

Active Disturbance Rejection Control treats the pendulum swing of the Kevlar cable as an external disturbance. An Extended State Observer estimates the swing state in real-time from IMU attitude rate data at 100 Hz — no physical pendulum model required. The controller cancels the disturbance via DRV8838 brake pauses and micro attitude corrections to the Pixhawk.

ESO input: IMU roll_rate_dps, pitch_rate_dps @ 100 Hz ESO output: estimated swing disturbance magnitude If disturbance > 8 dps → DRV8838 brake 300 ms AND → SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET opposing swing direction

Full Winch Sequence

A

Pre-lower stabilisation

GPS drift under 0.5 m/s, roll and pitch rates under 3 dps. Ensures cable starts near-vertical.

B

Soft ramp lower — 30% PWM over 10 steps × 50 ms = 500 ms

DRV8838 ramps from 0 to 30% duty. Cable unspools slowly. ADRC ESO begins reading IMU at 100 Hz.

C

Active swing damping during lower

If roll or pitch rate exceeds 8 dps: DRV8838 brakes for 300 ms, then resumes. Pixhawk receives micro attitude corrections opposing swing direction via SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET.

D

Ground contact detection — 180 mA stall current

N20 motor stalls on contact. DRV8838 current spikes above 180 mA. FSM detects spike and brakes immediately.

E

Payload release — CF mechanical release

Mechanical release actuates. Wait 500 ms. Confirm current drop (load gone) before commanding reel-back.

F

Soft ramp reel-back — 20% PWM over 10 steps

DRV8838 ramps from 0 to 20% duty in reverse. ADRC wobble guard continues. Free cable oscillates more — brake pause on any rate above 8 dps.

G

Spool home — hall effect or back-EMF

Cable fully retracted. DRV8838 brakes. Winch disarmed. FSM transitions to CG compensation sequence.

// Winch parameters
WINCH_LOWER_DUTY   = 30%   // PWM duty for lowering
WINCH_RETRACT_DUTY = 20%   // PWM duty for reel-back
RAMP_STEPS         = 10
RAMP_INTERVAL_MS   = 50    // 500 ms total ramp time
WOBBLE_LIMIT_DPS   = 8.0   // degrees per second
GROUND_CONTACT_MA  = 180   // milliamps stall threshold

lower sequence:
  wait_for_stable_hover()                 // gps drift < 0.5 m/s, rates < 3 dps
  ramp_drv8838(0, WINCH_LOWER_DUTY, RAMP_STEPS)
  while not ground_contact:
    if imu_rate > WOBBLE_LIMIT_DPS: brake(300ms) then resume
    if current > GROUND_CONTACT_MA: break
  brake_drv8838()
  actuate_mechanical_release()              // CF hook
  sleep(500ms); confirm_current_drop()
  ramp_drv8838(0, -WINCH_RETRACT_DUTY, RAMP_STEPS)
  while not spool_home:
    if imu_rate > WOBBLE_LIMIT_DPS: brake(300ms) then resume
  brake_drv8838();  winch_disarmed = true;
Section 06

Centre of Gravity Compensation After Payload Drop

When the 100 g payload is released, total mass drops by approximately 5.4% (100 g / 1850 g AUW). The CG shifts upward and potentially laterally. Without correction, the drone will pitch or roll briefly and the PID throttle tuning (calibrated with payload attached) will over-supply lift momentarily.

What happens at release

  • Total mass drops ~100 g (~5.4%)
  • CG shifts upward and possibly laterally
  • Throttle over-supplies lift momentarily
  • Attitude PID sees new dynamics — transient pitch/roll

Compensation approach

  • Pre-computed throttle feed-forward reduction
  • Attitude bias injection via SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET (2 s)
  • Pixhawk PID natural re-convergence
  • FSM stability gate before return flight

Step 1 + 2: Throttle Feed-Forward

// After payload release confirmed (current spike gone)
const float PAYLOAD_MASS_KG   = 0.100f;
const float DRONE_MASS_KG     = 1.850f;   // AUW with payload
const float mass_ratio_change = PAYLOAD_MASS_KG / DRONE_MASS_KG;  // ~5.4%

// Reduce throttle setpoint by mass ratio immediately
float new_throttle_ff = current_throttle_ff * (1.0f - mass_ratio_change);
send_throttle_feedforward(vehicle, new_throttle_ff);

Step 3: Attitude Bias Injection (2 seconds)

A SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET command is sent with pre-computed CG bias offsets as roll and pitch corrections for 2 seconds. This counteracts transient instability as the FC's PID re-converges on the new dynamic equilibrium.

Step 5: Stability Gate Before Return Flight

// Post-release stability gate — must pass before ascending for return
auto wait_for_cg_settle = [&](float timeout_s) {
    auto t_start = now();
    while (elapsed(t_start) < timeout_s) {
        float roll_rate  = fabsf(telem.attitude_angular_velocity().roll_rad_s  * 57.3f);
        float pitch_rate = fabsf(telem.attitude_angular_velocity().pitch_rad_s * 57.3f);
        float gps_speed  = hypotf(telem.velocity().north_m_s, telem.velocity().east_m_s);

        if (roll_rate < 3.0f && pitch_rate < 3.0f && gps_speed < 0.5f)
            return STABLE;

        std::this_thread::sleep_for(std::chrono::milliseconds(50));
    }
    return TIMEOUT;  // proceed anyway, log the event
};
Stability gate thresholds: Roll rate < 3 dps AND pitch rate < 3 dps AND GPS speed < 0.5 m/s. Wait 1.5 seconds post-release. Timeout allowed — event is logged but flight continues.
Section 07

Code Safety Mechanisms

1. Depth-Based Safety Zone Segmentation

The Myriad X generates a depth map every frame. The FSM classifies each frame into three zones. Red zone pixels from the HSV mask are overlaid on the depth map — visually restricted areas and physically obstructed areas are both represented in the same safety map the FSM reads every 30 ms cycle.

CLEAR

Depth > 5 m. Full cruise speed permitted (2 m/s).

CAUTION

Depth 2–5 m. Speed capped at 1 m/s.

DANGER

Depth < 2 m OR red pixel present. Full stop, re-evaluate.

// Safety zone classification per frame
enum SafeZone { CLEAR, CAUTION, DANGER };

SafeZone classify_zone(float depth_m, bool red_pixel_present) {
    if (red_pixel_present)      return DANGER;   // red zone overrides depth
    if (depth_m < 2.0f)        return DANGER;
    if (depth_m < 5.0f)        return CAUTION;
    return CLEAR;
}

float get_safe_speed(SafeZone z) {
    if (z == DANGER)   return 0.0f;
    if (z == CAUTION)  return 1.0f;
    return MAX_CRUISE_SPEED;  // 2.0 m/s
}

2. Auto Brightness and Saturation Normalisation (CLAHE)

The OAK-D Lite transitions from shaded corridor to open-sun delivery zone. Without correction, YOLO and HSV masks fail on over or under-exposed frames. CLAHE is applied to the L channel of LAB colour space (preserving hue), followed by auto-saturation scaling in HSV. Runs on TX2 NX CUDA before the frame enters the Myriad X inference queue.

// Brightness normalisation before inference (TX2 NX CUDA)
cv::Mat normalize_frame(const cv::Mat& bgr) {
    cv::Mat lab;
    cv::cvtColor(bgr, lab, cv::COLOR_BGR2Lab);

    std::vector<cv::Mat> channels;
    cv::split(lab, channels);

    // CLAHE on L channel only — preserves hue for HSV masks
    auto clahe = cv::createCLAHE(/*clipLimit=*/2.0, cv::Size(8, 8));
    clahe->apply(channels[0], channels[0]);
    cv::merge(channels, lab);
    cv::cvtColor(lab, result, cv::COLOR_Lab2BGR);

    // Auto saturation: if washed out (<60), scale up to 1.5×
    cv::Mat hsv;
    cv::cvtColor(result, hsv, cv::COLOR_HSV2BGR);
    cv::split(hsv, channels);
    float mean_sat = cv::mean(channels[1])[0];
    if (mean_sat < 60.0f) {
        float scale = std::min(1.5f, 80.0f / mean_sat);
        channels[1].convertTo(channels[1], -1, scale, 0);
    }
    cv::merge(channels, hsv);
    cv::cvtColor(hsv, result, cv::COLOR_HSV2BGR);
    return result;
}

3. Wall Detection via Depth Map Segmentation

Used in the forward lap corridor. The TX2 NX builds a 2D lateral distance profile by sampling the depth map at fixed horizontal slices. If asymmetry exceeds 0.3 m, a lateral correction velocity is applied.

// Lateral wall balance (forward lap only)
float left_dist  = median_depth_in_region(depth, LEFT_COLUMN_ROI);
float right_dist = median_depth_in_region(depth, RIGHT_COLUMN_ROI);
float lateral_err = left_dist - right_dist;
float vy_cmd      = -0.3f * lateral_err;
mavsdk.send_velocity(FWD_SPEED, vy_cmd, 0.0f);

Section 09

Autonomous Return and Landing

The return lap activates the full obstacle navigation pipeline — this is the key difference from the forward lap, which used corridor wall-following only.

Post-delivery ascent 10 m→ GPS to corridor exit→ Banner detect + align→ Descend to 3 m→ A-star obstacle nav→ GPS to take-off point→ LAND mode · disarm

Visual SLAM in the corridor: RTAB-Map or NVBlox running on TX2 NX constructs a real-time 3D map from OAK-D stereo RGB-D and transmits Visual Odometry to the Pixhawk over MAVLink. Used as GPS substitute where satellite multipath errors are elevated inside the corridor structure.

Landing: Pixhawk LAND mode. Barometer primary for final 1 m. Motors disarm on touchdown (accelerometer impact threshold).

Section 10

Emergency Override and RTL

Under normal flight the Pixhawk enforces 60% maximum throttle. Six conditions override this cap and restore full authority:

ConditionTrigger ThresholdOverride Action
Battery criticalBelow 3.5V per cell (21V for 6S)Full throttle, immediate RTL
GPS signal lostNo fix for 3 secondsFull throttle, ascend to 15 m, hold or RTL
Geofence breachPosition outside programmed boundaryFull throttle, Pixhawk corrects back inside
Datalink lost (primary)No Skydroid T12 / TX2 NX MAVLink heartbeat for 1.5 s3DR backup link active — GCS maintains RTL authority. If 3DR also lost: Pixhawk standalone RTL
Dual camera failureBoth OAK-D and Arducam streams lostFull throttle, RTL immediately
Manual emergencySkydroid T12 channel 5 switchedFull throttle, full pilot authority in Stabilize
RTL sequence: Climb to 15 m at full allowed throttle → fly directly to stored take-off GPS → descend and land → disarm on touchdown. The 60% cap is restored only after manual re-arm in normal mode.
Section 11

Summary of Innovations

01 — Dual-processor vision pipeline

Myriad X performs all inference. TX2 NX never receives a full raw frame — only results via USB 3.0. Eliminates GPU/CPU contention. Guarantees 30 ms cycle.

02 — Adaptive resolution at fixed 30 fps

4K → 1080p → 720p based on combined quality score (ZBar + depth confidence). Frame rate held constant. Runtime nvvidconv cap switch — no pipeline restart.

03 — ADRC winch without pendulum model

ESO estimates swing disturbance from IMU attitude rate signals. No physical pendulum model. Robust to cable length changes between deployment heights.

04 — Depth + red zone safety map fusion

HSV red zone mask overlaid on stereo depth map. Single FSM safety classification (CLEAR/CAUTION/DANGER) unifies obstacle avoidance and zone avoidance logic.

05 — CUDA CLAHE brightness normalisation

CLAHE on LAB L-channel preserves hue for HSV masks while correcting local brightness. Auto-saturation scaling restores discriminability on over-exposed frames.

06 — CG compensation and stability gate

Throttle feed-forward + attitude bias injection immediately post-drop. FSM stability gate holds until rates and GPS speed confirm re-convergence before return flight.

07 — ADRC wind compensation at 10 m hover

Linear ESO estimates wind gust forces on drone body. Proactive corrections before drift occurs. Combined with Pixhawk FC weathervaning for layered rejection.

08 — Visual SLAM as GPS substitute

RTAB-Map / NVBlox on TX2 NX transmits visual odometry to Pixhawk via MAVLink. Used as GPS substitute inside the corridor where multipath errors are elevated.

09 — Five-trigger camera failover

USB timeout, VPU overtemp, decode fail streak, depth confidence drop, angular rate excess — seamless switch to Arducam B0251, auto-recovery every 30 seconds.

10 — Emergency RTL with full throttle authority

Pixhawk retains independent RTL authority at full throttle even if TX2 NX is unresponsive. Six trigger conditions. 60% cap restored only after manual re-arm.

11 — Ground contact via motor stall current

DRV8838 current spike above 180 mA on N20 stall. No load cell or limit switch needed. Eliminates weight and complexity while meeting rulebook release requirement.

12 — Payload safety via offboard monitoring

The offboard computer is dedicated strictly to payload safety checks (verifying payload existence and proper release). Heavy computing is kept onboard due to bandwidth limits.

13 — Backup 3DR telemetry for FC health checks

Independent 915 MHz MAVLink link provides FC health checks and monitors winch state/payload release confirmation. It avoids camera transmission to stay within bandwidth limits.
